## Change Management Rules

Every significant change must include:

1. Date of change.
2. Changed document or component.
3. Reason for change.
4. Technical impact.
5. Compatibility considerations.
6. Related lesson or phase.

Curriculum changes must preserve the approved learning sequence unless explicitly approved by the student.

Package updates must be checked against official release notes and NuGet compatibility information.

Completed lesson documents must not be silently rewritten in a way that invalidates previous exercises or assessments.
